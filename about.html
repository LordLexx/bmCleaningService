About us

The people behind the polish.

A Maidenhead-based cleaning company built on reliability, friendliness and an obsessive eye for detail.

Our story

Clean makes a difference.

At B&M Cleaning Services, we believe that a clean and well-maintained space makes a real difference. Our aim is simple: to provide reliable, professional and high-quality cleaning that helps our customers enjoy fresh, comfortable and welcoming spaces.

We understand that every home and business is different, which is why we take the time to understand your individual needs and tailor the service around them. Whether it’s regular domestic cleaning, a one-off deep clean, an end-of-tenancy handover or professional cleaning for your business, every job gets the same care and professionalism.

We take pride in paying attention to the details that are often overlooked — from everyday surfaces to those harder-to-reach spots. Our goal is always the same: to leave every space looking clean, fresh and cared for.
