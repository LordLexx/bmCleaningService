What we do

One company for every kind of clean.

From a weekly once-over to a full end-of-tenancy blitz — pick a service to see exactly what's included. Every price is confirmed upfront, in writing, before anyone picks up a cloth.

Weekly or fortnightly visits from your own regular cleaner. Dusting, hoovering, mopping, kitchens and bathrooms — the full routine, done properly, on a day that suits you.

  • Kitchen surfaces, sink & stovetop
  • Bathrooms descaled & disinfected
  • Hoovered throughout, hard floors mopped
  • Dusting — sills, skirtings, shelves, lamps
  • Bins emptied & liners replaced
  • Ironing on request (add 30 minutes)

The once- or twice-a-year reset. We go everywhere the weekly clean doesn't — behind, on top of, and inside the things you'd rather not think about.

  • Inside all kitchen cabinets & drawers
  • Oven, extractor & fridge deep-cleaned
  • Limescale & grout treatment
  • Skirting boards, frames & light switches
  • Interior windows & mirrors
  • Behind & under movable furniture

Landlord- and letting-agent-approved cleans, worked through a 60-point inventory checklist. Book with your move date and we'll handle the rest — including key collection if you've already left.

  • 60-point checklist, photographed on completion
  • Carpets steam-cleaned (optional add-on)
  • Oven & all appliances, inside and out
  • Walls, skirtings & woodwork wiped down
  • 48-hour re-clean guarantee if your agent flags anything
  • Certificate of completion for your landlord

Building dust gets everywhere — including places builders swear don't exist. We do the fine-detail clean that turns a building site back into a home.

  • Fine dust extraction — every surface & crevice
  • Paint splatter & adhesive removal
  • Windows, frames & sills, in and out where safe
  • Full deep clean of kitchen & bathrooms
  • Builders' rubble bagged for collection
  • Air out & final polish throughout

Offices, surgeries, salons and studios across Berkshire. Out-of-hours cleaning with secure key-holding, so your team walks into a fresh space every morning.

  • Early-morning or evening shifts, 7 days a week
  • Key-holding & alarm-code protocols
  • Kitchens, washrooms & communal areas
  • Consumables restocked at cost
  • Same dedicated team, full holiday cover
  • Monthly invoice — no surprises

Why B&M

Anyone can wave a duster around. We clean like we mean it.

B&M is Becky and Mark — a husband-and-wife team from Reading. When you ring, one of us answers. When we say Tuesday, we mean Tuesday.

01

Fully insured

£5 million public liability cover plus key-holding insurance. Your home, your keys and your peace of mind are properly protected — certificate available on request.

02

Vetted & DBS-checked

We interview in person, chase references and run DBS checks before anyone cleans solo. Roughly one in ten applicants makes it onto our team — we're fussy so you don't have to be.

03

The same face every visit

No rotating rosters. Your cleaner gets to know your home — and where you keep the hoover bags — so the standard never slips from week one to week a hundred.

The B&M promise — if something isn't right, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and sort it, free of charge. No quibbles, no small print.

How it works

Sorted in three straightforward steps.

  1. 1

    STEP 01

    Tell us about your place

    Two minutes on the phone or the form below. We'll talk through what you need and give you a straight answer on price — no hard sell, no "free assessment" that turns into a consultation.

  2. 2

    STEP 02

    Meet your cleaner

    We'll match you with a cleaner who suits your home and routine, then come along for the first visit together. You'll always know exactly who's holding your keys.

  3. 3

    STEP 03

    Get on with your day

    Same cleaner, same day, every week. Pay by standing order or card, pause for holidays, and cancel any time with a week's notice. That's genuinely it.
